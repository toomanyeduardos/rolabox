Delete your Rolabox account

Rolabox, by Eduardo Flores

An account is optional in Rolabox. If you have one, you can delete it, with all the data synced to it, from the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Rolabox, signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete account, at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm it's you: enter your password, or choose your Google account, whichever the account uses. Your phone needs an internet connection.
  5. Select Delete account to confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted right away. Then Rolabox erases everything it keeps on your phone and closes. The next time you open it, it starts as a fresh install. Your music files are yours and aren't touched.

Without the app

If you can't use the app, for example because you no longer have the phone, email rolabox.app@gmail.com from the email address of the account, asking for it to be deleted. If you signed in with Google, that's your Google account's email address. Your account and its data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your account: its email address, its name and profile photo link if you signed in with Google, and its account ID.
  • Your synced settings.
  • The podcasts you follow.
  • When you delete in the app, also everything Rolabox keeps on that phone: its library, settings, podcasts, downloads and covers.

What isn't deleted

  • Crash reports. They aren't linked to your account, so they can't be found by it. They're deleted after 90 days.
  • Rolabox's data on your other phones. If you were signed in on another phone, its local data stays there until you clear Rolabox's storage or uninstall it. It's signed out and no longer syncs.
  • Android backups. A backup of Rolabox's settings and podcasts in your Google account is kept and managed by Android, not by Rolabox, under your phone's backup settings.

Nothing is kept on Rolabox's side after an account is deleted.

More about the data Rolabox handles is in the privacy policy.
