3Podcasts: browse, play, search, follow, mobile data (22–37)
Levels
Podcasts → Owner → Show → Episode → Now Playing. Search is always the first row of Podcasts, so it's one turn away with 0 shows or 100. 09 shows the same list while music plays.
Follow (proposal)
The first row of every show, with On or Off on the right, changed in place by center like a setting. Off: the show lists only what you've downloaded. On: new episodes are listed each time the app checks (on open, with a connection). Nothing downloads on its own; an episode downloads only when you play it. Playing one episode from search adds the show with Follow Off.
Episode rows
Newest first. Full dot: unplayed. Half dot: partly played. No dot: played. Right side: a percentage while downloading, an arrow when not downloaded yet. Dots are orange, as in 09 and the rule that orange marks unplayed. Dots only show in your library, not on shows opened from search.
Episode screen
Center on an episode opens its screen: Play (or Resume and Play from Start), then the date, length and description. Turning past the last row scrolls the text. ⏮ ⏭ during an episode: tap skips back 15 s or forward 30 s, hold scrubs.
Search and the keyboard
Opening Search shows the phone's keyboard in place of the wheel. Results update as you type: Shows (name or creator), then Episodes (title or description). The keyboard's search key, or Android back, hides it and gives the wheel the first result. MENU goes back to typing. Section labels are skipped by the wheel.
Mobile data (35–37)
Settings → Podcasts → Use Mobile Data, On by default. Off: episodes download only on Wi-Fi, and playing one that isn't downloaded waits for Wi-Fi. Search and the new-episode check always run on mobile data. Already downloaded episodes always play.
1.5x text
Rows, labels, the search field and Now Playing scale with the system font. The list shows fewer rows and scrolls. Now Playing puts the art above the text at a smaller size, and the title wraps to 2 lines. The keyboard is Android's and follows its own settings.
Stream while downloading
Play starts the download and plays from it. On Now Playing the bar's lighter part is what's downloaded, and DOWNLOADING with a percentage replaces the right label until it's done.

2Music library states: permission, scan, no music, reading tags (15–19)
Where
Each state takes the place of the Music menu, under the "Music" header. The body and wheel don't change. MENU goes back. ⏮ ⏭ ⏯ keep controlling whatever is loaded.
First words (15, 16)
Both rows start with "Allow music access", which makes sense on its own. At 1.5x the row still shows "Allow music access to…" or "…in…" before the ellipsis. Highlighted, it scrolls to the end, as every row does (ADR-018).
No › (15, 16)
Left off. › means center opens another device screen. Here center brings up Android's prompt over this screen (15) or leaves the app for system settings (16), so a › would promise something that doesn't happen.
Tag bar (19)
A 3px gray line, edge to edge along the bottom of the display, with square ends. Now Playing's bar is 6px, inset, rounded, with times under it and a knob when scrubbing. This one has no times or knob and only appears on lists, never on Now Playing.
Tag bar at 1.5x
The bar gets its own strip under the list, and the list's area ends above it. If the rows don't fit, the list scrolls inside that area as usual, so the bar never covers a row and the wheel reaches every row.

1Device screen: display + wheel
Two parts
The display (top) is the only area that changes. The aluminum body and the wheel stay fixed. Menus slide in from the right when you go deeper and slide out when you press MENU.
Wheel
Turn: move the highlight (in menus) or change the volume (on Now Playing). Center: select. MENU: back. ⏮ ⏭: previous/next track. ⏯: play/pause. A haptic tick fires on each highlight change.
Status
The display stays white on both finishes, so text reads the same on either. The vinyl in the display header spins while audio plays. It's hidden before anything is loaded. Playback continues while you browse (see 09).
Shuffle and Repeat (20, 21)
Icons sit right after "3 OF 10", since both describe the queue, in display ink at 12px tall. The header already holds the vinyl, title and battery, and under the bar are the times. VOLUME and SCRUB keep the right side. Shuffle shows while Shuffle is Songs or Albums, and on a queue started by Shuffle Songs whatever the setting says. Repeat One replaces Repeat. Off shows nothing.
Orange
The highlighted row is glossy blue, with a lighter top half and a darker bottom half. Orange, the brand color, is only used for unplayed dots, the scrub knob and the vinyl label.
