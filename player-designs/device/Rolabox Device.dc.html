1Device screen: display + wheel
Two parts
The display (top) is the only area that changes. The aluminum body and the wheel stay fixed. Menus slide in from the right when you go deeper and slide out when you press MENU.
Wheel
Turn: move the highlight (in menus) or change the volume (on Now Playing). Center: select. MENU: back. ⏮ ⏭: previous/next track. ⏯: play/pause. A haptic tick fires on each highlight change.
Status
The display stays white on both finishes, so text reads the same on either. The vinyl in the display header spins while audio plays. It's hidden before anything is loaded. Playback continues while you browse (see 09).
Orange
The highlighted row is glossy blue, with a lighter top half and a darker bottom half. Orange, the brand color, is only used for unplayed dots, the scrub knob and the vinyl label.
